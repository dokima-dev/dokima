**The four stories:**

2. **A story waits unplanned while its files are in flight.** It waits if it shares a file with running work's plan or with an open pull request at the merge gate. 3. **A waiting story plans once the work ahead ends.** That work merging or closing starts its planner on the new main. (2 criteria; depends on 2)
4. **A plan reaching outside its story's files is checked again.** If that plan needs a file another change in flight is touching, the story waits before its worker starts. Its worker then starts once that work merges or closes. (2 criteria; depends on 2 and 3)
