**Agent:** I changed `file_split` in `dokima/agent.py`. A split that is approved again after its stories were filed now files only its new stories, and stories already filed are not filed a second time. The plan's test and the other split tests pass (3 passed). The hand-back checks also pass.

- **Why it used to fail:** `file_split` handed back the old split record whenever one existed, so a re-approved split filed nothing.
- **How it decides now:** it hands back an existing split record only when that record is newer than the newest plan. So filing the same re-plan twice still files nothing more.
- **Stories kept from the earlier split:** a story is matched to an earlier one by its title. A match reuses the issue already filed and is marked kept, so it is not filed again and its blocked-by links are not added again. New stories can still be blocked by kept ones.
- **What else changed in the split command:**
  - The board leaves kept stories where they are.
  - Autopilot starts planning only the new stories that wait on nothing.
  - A record of the new filing is posted even when an earlier split record exists.

Two limits you should know about:

- A kept story whose title was reworded in the re-plan counts as new and gets filed again.
- A story dropped from the re-plan keeps its open sub-issue; nothing closes it.
